SUPPORT SERVICES

How can we help?

Explore the different areas of support available to students.

💻

IT Support

IT support can help students with common technology challenges.

  • Computer problems
  • Internet and network problems
  • Student account issues
  • Basic software problems

Example: If a student cannot connect to the university network, they can seek appropriate IT support.

📚

Academic Support

Academic support can help students improve their learning and study practices.

  • Study skills
  • Academic resources
  • Research guidance
  • Time management
📖

Library Support

The library provides resources that can help students with research and learning.

  • Books and reference materials
  • Research resources
  • Online learning resources
  • Study spaces
🌱

Student Wellness

Wellness involves taking care of yourself while managing university responsibilities.

  • Healthy study habits
  • Rest and breaks
  • Managing academic pressure
  • Knowing when to seek appropriate support
